To Markdown

Turn a PDF, Word, Excel or CSV file into Markdown, edit it, then download it as .md or as a Word file.

  • PDF: works for PDFs that contain text. Headings are guessed from font size. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) need OCR and are not supported; tables and multi-column pages come out as plain text.
  • Word (.docx): headings, lists, tables, bold and italic are kept. Images are skipped.
  • Excel and CSV: every sheet becomes a table (first 2,000 rows).

Files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded. Check the result: automatic conversion is never perfect.
