About

Biology graduate from Banda Aceh, working with satellite imagery.

I’m from Sigli and live in Banda Aceh, Aceh. I studied Biology at the Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences (FMIPA), Universitas Syiah Kuala.

Remote sensing runs through both my internship and my thesis. I’m now building on it with GIS, ArcGIS Pro and Python.

Thesis

Analisis Sebaran Vegetasi Menggunakan Nilai Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) di Kota Banda Aceh, Provinsi Aceh

The results are summarised on the home page.

How the analysis was done

  1. Sentinel-2A imagery for 2019 and 2023.
  2. Google Earth Engine calculates NDVI.
  3. GeoTIFF export of the NDVI result.
  4. ArcGIS 10.8 clips the area, reclassifies the values and runs the spatial analysis.
