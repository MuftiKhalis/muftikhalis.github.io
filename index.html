Reading vegetation from space, one 10-metre pixel at a time.

I’m Mufti Khalis, a biology graduate from Universitas Syiah Kuala in Banda Aceh. This site holds my remote sensing work, a few small tools, and English study links.

Banda Aceh’s vegetation cover fell by one percentage point from 2019 to 2023.

My thesis compared Sentinel-2A imagery from both years using NDVI. Vegetation covered 52% of the city in 2019 and 51% in 2023. Non-vegetation grew from 48% (2,729.27 ha) to 49% (2,808.19 ha).

Vegetation was lowest in the dense city centre and higher where green open space remained.
